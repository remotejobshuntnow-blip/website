PR Coverage Hub saves you hours every week by automating your PR reporting workflow. We're 2x more affordable than competitors like CoverageBook, starting at just $25/month. With automated metrics, instant screenshots, and customizable branding, you'll deliver stunning reports that make you look like a PR rockstar. What metrics do you collect automatically?
We automatically collect comprehensive metrics for every coverage piece you add: reach and impressions to show your audience size, domain authority to demonstrate outlet credibility, backlink data to track SEO value, and social shares to measure engagement. All metrics are gathered instantly when you add a URL—no manual data entry, no spreadsheets, no headaches. Just paste your coverage links and watch the data populate automatically.
Do you just collect metrics, or do you do more?
We do so much more! From a single URL, we capture full-page screenshots (without ads and popups—even from behind paywalls), extract article titles, pull publication dates, and compile all your metrics automatically. Then we assemble everything into a beautiful, client-ready report. You can customize it with your branding, add your logo and brand colors, and deliver it as a polished PDF or share it online. Or simply export all the data to use however you wish. It's a complete end-to-end solution for PR reporting.
How can I trust the data you provide?
We partner with industry-leading third-party data providers to ensure accuracy and reliability. Our metrics come from trusted sources like SimilarWeb for outlet audience data and Moz for Domain Authority scores—the same sources used by top PR agencies and marketing teams worldwide. We don't make up numbers; we pull real, verified data from established industry authorities so you can report with confidence.
How many templates are available, and can I customize them?
We offer an array of professionally designed templates to choose from, each crafted to match different brand personalities and needs. Every template is fully customizable—add your logo, apply your brand colors, and tailor the layout to match your client's style. Switch between templates instantly to find the perfect fit for each campaign or client.
What's included in the 14-day free trial?
Your 14-day free trial gives you full access to all features—no credit card required! It's a risk-free way to see how much time PR Coverage Hub can save you. After your trial, choose the plan that fits your needs: Bronze for solo PR pros, Silver for small teams, or Gold for large agencies.
Can I track coverage across different media channels?
Absolutely! PR Coverage Hub supports multi-channel tracking so you can compile online articles, print publications, broadcast segments, and social media placements all in a single professional report. No more juggling separate spreadsheets for different media types—everything lives in one place, beautifully organized and ready to showcase to your clients or leadership team.
